Let's verify it's you

Good news, we already have your BVN on file. We just need to confirm your ID and take a quick selfie.

⏱ Takes about 3 minutes
  • 🪪 A valid ID, NIN, driver's license or voter's card
  • 🤳 A few seconds for a selfie
Why confirm my identity if you have my BVN? ＋

Your BVN confirms who you were when you registered, it doesn't confirm it's really you applying right now. The ID photo and live selfie prove that, so no one else can take out a loan using your details.
